Harena Term — terminal sample.  `cat docs/sample.txt`
Each block says what to look at. Set your terminal to this font first.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. HANGUL SPACING — the reason this font exists
   Compare against your previous font at the same size. The gaps between
   syllables should be noticeably tighter, and the syllables noticeably larger
   in the cell. Nothing should touch.

한글 자간은 원본 글꼴의 것을 그대로 가져옵니다
서울 부산 대구 인천 광주 대전 울산 세종 제주
빨간 우체통 앞에서 왼쪽으로 꺾어 쭉 걸으면 됩니다
뚫훓쫓빻꿇렬끓쏟짧웅쌍핥읊꽃잎쐐횃뷁훽쒫뾹      ← densest syllables; check for collisions

2. HANGUL BESIDE KANA — fixed in this build, and worth checking (ADR 0014)
   Hangul should read slightly heavier than the kana beside it, the way
   Pretendard draws it. v0.9.0 had this the wrong way round. Kana still
   looks a little larger — it is drawn wide and carries fewer strokes —
   but it should no longer look bolder.

로그인 ログイン 되었습니다 サーバー 응답 レスポンス
가나다라마바사 アイウエオカキ あいうえおかき

3. K vs J REGIONAL FORMS — 611 codepoints differ
   Harena Term K keeps the radical's traditional two dots; J uses one.
   Set K and J in turn and watch the lower left of each character.

運 進 週 選 過 達 通 連 遠 道 近 返 退 追 迎 逆 造 速 適
週末に運転して遠くの道を通り、近くの店に返品した。

   Both lines must look identical. If the second shows stacked or overlapping
   jamo, the font is not composing and the shaper is not covering for it.

   Bold hangul should stay inside its cell. Nothing should touch its neighbour.
